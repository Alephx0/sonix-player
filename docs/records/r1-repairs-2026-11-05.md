normal recovery updater. The R1 returned to its normal rootfs and launched
`/usr/bin/sonix_player` under `/usr/bin/sonix_launch`; its installed MD5 exactly
matches the accepted temporary executable. Post-boot screenshots show the Library
and profile picture; input-event taps opened Liked Songs from its cover, and a
